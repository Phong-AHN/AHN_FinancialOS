AHN Financial Operating System / AI CFO — Software Requirements 1. Product Goal
Build a financial operating system for AHN that tracks every single dollar coming into and leaving the company, across all connected accounts, entities, projects, events, employees, vendors, subscriptions, payroll systems, and banking platforms.

The system must answer, in real time:

How much cash do we have?
Where is every dollar coming from?
Where is every dollar going?
What is our monthly burn?
How much runway do we have?
How much revenue do we need this month to break even?
How much revenue do we need to hit a target profit margin or growth rate?
Which projects, clients, business units, events, and services are profitable?
Which subscriptions or vendors have increased in price?
Which software or recurring expenses can be reduced, replaced, consolidated, or eliminated?
What financial changes require immediate attention?
Core non-negotiable requirement: every dollar must be traceable from company-level reporting down to the underlying bank transaction, payment, vendor, employee, client, project, event, invoice, or subscription responsible for it.

4. Every-Dollar Alerting
Default alert mode: every dollar.

Whenever money enters or leaves any connected account, the system should be capable of sending an alert through:

Slack
SMS/text message
Email
In-app notifications
Example inflow alert:
+$12,500 received from Client X. Project: Website Redesign. Account: US Operating. Current total cash: $284,300.

Example outflow alert:
-$4,800 sent through VEEM for Philippines payroll. Monthly payroll spend is now $31,400. Current runway: 7.8 months.

Users must be able to configure alerts by transaction amount, inflow/outflow, account, vendor, category, project, event, business unit, subscription, payroll, price increase, bank balance threshold, runway threshold, profitability threshold, overdue invoice, budget overrun, or unusual transaction.

Support immediate alerts, daily summaries, weekly summaries, and digest modes.

28. Critical Acceptance Criteria
The application is not complete unless:

Every imported dollar can be traced to its source transaction.
No transaction is double-counted when the same activity appears in a bank feed and QuickBooks.
Cash balances reconcile with connected financial accounts.
User can receive every-dollar inflow/outflow notifications via Slack, SMS, and email.
User can see runway based on actual cash and burn.
User can see required monthly break-even revenue.
User can model monthly revenue growth and target margins.
User can see profitability for every project and event.
Labor/time can be incorporated into project profitability.
Subscription price changes and recurring expenses are automatically flagged.
User can drill from company-level KPIs down to individual transactions.
The system maintains an audit trail of financial-data changes.
29. Product Principle
This is not intended to replace accounting software. QuickBooks remains the ledger/accounting system. This product is the financial intelligence, forecasting, cost-control, project-profitability, and executive-alert system sitting above the ledger.

North-star product requirement: Every dollar in. Every dollar out. Always know what changed, why it changed, where the money went, how profitable the activity was, and how much runway remains.
